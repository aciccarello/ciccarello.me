TIL that Connecticut's 8 counties were officially abolished from government in 1960 and that they primarily only are used as legacy geographical boundaries. In the 1980s the state realized they probably DID need some kind of regional divisions for permitting and established the "Councils of governments" which have since been simplified to 9 regions and been recognized as county equivalents.
