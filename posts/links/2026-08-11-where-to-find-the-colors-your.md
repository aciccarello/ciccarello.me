I've read articles on color spaces before and was aware of limitations with digital screens but this was the clearest explanation I've read. I love that it focuses on the unique colors of the natural world, both that we can see and some we can't. If you haven't heard of the structural ways some bird feathers get their color, there's a section on that too. It will definitely get me to pay more attention to the greens and blues in the forest, birds, water, and even traffic lights.
