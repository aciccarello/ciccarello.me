While I can only absorb so much of Dallas Willard's logic when listening to the audiobook, I do appreciate its main thesis: Christian apologetics should focus on dealing with doubts and challenges to faith rather than arguing unbelievers into logical submission.
This book was shorter and less dense than Willard's well known "The Divine Conspiracy" and so I'd probably recommend it to someone interested in getting a taste of his theology.
There were some parts that got into more into the logical arguments traditional to apologetics but focused more on showing how the world we live in is consistent with Willard's view of God.
I also appreciated the ending chapter on hearing from God as a way to transition from the theoretical into the more practical.
