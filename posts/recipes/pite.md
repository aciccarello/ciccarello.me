In 2026 I found this recipe in a printed panflet someone gave me when I visited Kosovo in 2014.
I seem to remember it was something the students of the school put together, possibly as an English practice exercise.
As such, I've tried to keep the original text as much as possible with a few changes like breaking it into a list and adding the amounts.

The recipe is included with the following description:

> ## Recipe for pite with spinach
> This pite is one of the most scrumptious pites from Kosova's kitchen. It's very delicious and healthy.

I also remember going over someone's house who served spanakopita and loving it.
It's been a long time, but I liked the idea of making it again after all these years.
I'm not sure that I'll be able to reproduce a thin filo, but the instructions seem simple enough.
[This Kosovo Pita recipe](https://culinarycreationss.com/recipe/kosovo-pita-recipe-cheese-pie), while not a spinach pie, has some helpful tips like, like keeping the sheets of dough covered with a damp cloth.
Based on my research, I suspect the cheese is either cottege cheese or feta cheese. I've also seen some people recommend using scallions instead of or with the onion.
