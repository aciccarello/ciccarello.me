Daily Prayer

Help me pay attention today. I want to be so acutely aware of Your presence in every space. Help me notice beauty that doesn't beg for applause, conversations that carry Your kindness, and the still, small ways You're working beneath the surface. Let my feet be rooted, not rushed. Let my heart be soft, not guarded. And let my soul remember that You waste nothing—not even times of slowing down.

Practicing the Way: Rule of Life

A Rule of Life is a helpful tool to organize your life in order to live intentionally in your apprenticeship to Jesus. I've selected these practices to help shape me towards Christ's example. See the Rule of Life Builder for a guided experience to create your own Rule of Life.

Category Daily Weekly
Sabbath
  • Spend time in the quiet
  • Observe a 24-hour Sabbath
  • Set aside time to enjoy beautiful things (art/nature)
Prayer
  • Pray daily prayer
  • Spend time listening to God
  • Intercede for others
  • Petition God with your needs
Fasting
  • Replace a meal with giving the money I would have spent on food
Solitude
  • Have a technology-free hour before bed
  • Schedule 10-minute windows of stillness
  • Schedule one night of no activities
Generosity
  • Tithe
  • Give to a ministry, charity, or cause
  • Prayerfully review the week's spending
Scripture
  • Listen to Scripture
  • Listen to a teaching
Community
  • Attend a Sunday gathering
  • Call, visit, or write a note to a loved one
Service
  • Volunteer at your church or a local nonprofit
Witness
  • Pray for coworkers and leadership
  • Schedule margin in your schedule for God to move
Self Care
  • Follow a wind-down routine before bed
  • Sleep seven to nine hours a night
  • Spend time in nature
  • Attend a support group
